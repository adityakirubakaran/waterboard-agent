[page 2] Revocation Process
Division Staff may contact you for more information to determine whether the water right can be fully or partially revoked. If additional information is not provided within 45 days of Staff’s request, the Revocation Request will be cancelled. Division Staff may need to inspect the place of use and point of diversion. Water rights will be revoked when there is no existing diversion (or threat of diversion) that is subject to the permitting authority of the State Water Board. Please note, you must have submitted all required annual reports of diversion for this right before being eligible for a revocation.
All Water Right Owners Must Sign This Form

[page 2] All Water Right Owners Must Sign This Form
Please review your water right ownership information in eWRIMS to make sure all owners have signed this form. Revocations can only be initiated when all water right owners sign this form (an attorney may sign on behalf of trustees or corporations if legally entitled to do so). There may be additional owners of the water right who are not listed in our records. This often happens when our records were never updated to reflect all parcel owners and when there are multiple parcels covered under the place of use of the water right. Division staff will notify you if the water right has additional owners not listed in eWRIMS.

Unauthorized diversion and use of water is considered a trespass and subject to enforcement action under Water Code section 1052 and 1831. It is my (our) responsibility to consult with the California Department of Fish and Wildlife, the Regional Water Quality Control Board and, if a jurisdictional size dam will be removed or breached, the Department of Water Resources and Division of Safety of Dams, to ensure that removal of project facilities, if necessary, does not adversely affect a fishery or result in unregulated sediment discharge to a waterway.

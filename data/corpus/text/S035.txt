Specialized Training Providers
Per regulations, a specialized training course is defined as a college-level course providing at least 36 contact hours of training in drinking water or wastewater quality, drinking water or wastewater treatment, drinking water distribution or drinking water or wastewater facility operation, offered by an accredited academic institution or an organization either accredited by the International Association of Continuing Education Training (IACET) or an authorized IACET provider. The following list represents courses the State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB) has reviewed and previously accepted as specialized training courses for our drinking water distribution and treatment exams. If a course is not / no longer at least 36 hours, or if it is an exam prep course, it will not be accepted as a specialized training course. For reference, 3.6 Continuing Education Units (CEUs) is equivalent to 36 contact hours. Please verify course length before committing to the class.
An "X" in the DWT column means the course counts as a drinking water treatment (DWT) OR a DW distribution course.

[page 1] An "X" in the DWT column means the course counts as a drinking water treatment (DWT) OR a DW distribution course.
An "X" in the WSP column means the course counts as a water supply principles (DW distribution) course.
An "X" in the SUPPL column means the course counts only as a supplemental course required for Grade 3-5 exams.
Some providers are noted as “NOT CURRENT” and may no longer be offering specialized training courses.
Any courses listed in RED are “archived” and no longer offered by the provider but are still acceptable for exam qualification.

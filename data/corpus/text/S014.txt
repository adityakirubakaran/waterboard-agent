## Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook > Policy Handbook Updates
The Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook (CCCPH) took effect July 1, 2024. A FAQ dated June 27, 2025 is posted on the Guidance Documents.
The State Water Board adopted a revised version of the CCCPH on, April 21,2026, which corrected references to accrediting entities and permits the use of some existing protections for auxiliary water supplies. The following are an updated CCCPH and log of all changes made:
- April 2026 Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook (Word)(pdf)
- April 2026 Change Log (Word)(pdf)
The Cross-Connection Control Committee has released the following guidance document on the fire sprinkler requirements in the CCCPH.
- Guidance Memorandum on Fire Protection Systems, dated September 5, 2025 (pdf)
The Committee has released four Cross-Connection Control Plan (CCC Plan) templates, one for transient noncommunity water systems, one for nontransient noncommunity water systems, and two for small community water systems with less than 1,000 connections. The Committee has also prepared a training video on the CCCPH for water systems and stakeholders, linked below under Other Guidance Documents.

## Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook > Policy Handbook Updates
All Cross-Connection Control Plans, requests for extensions, or questions on Plans from public water systems must be submitted to their assigned DDW District Office or LPA. Comments and inquiries regarding the Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook may be directed to: backflow@waterboards.ca.gov

Acceptable Safety Continuing Education Topics
You are not required to submit safety classes for your renewal. However, if they are included, no more than 25% of your required hours may be safety related. All classes, presentations and meetings must be a minimum of 50 minutes long and multiple short classes over different days cannot be added together to equal one contact hour.

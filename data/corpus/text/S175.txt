## Wastewater Operator Certification Program > Forms
Please Note: These forms are PDF (portable document format) files that support filing-out the form on your home computer and printing it. IT DOES NOT support saving your filled-out form for later retrieval. Tabs are built-in for your convenience.
Make sure you are using the correct application. Using an incorrect application may delay the processing of your application. If you have problems using these applications, or would like it faxed or emailed to you, please call our general message line at (916) 341-5819 and leave your name and address or fax number.

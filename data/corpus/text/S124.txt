If the Permit, License or Application includes both direct diversion and storage, the two amounts will be additive, unless a total annual amount is specified. For water rights that are primarily for a hydropower purpose, the fee shall be 1) the greater of either 30 percent of the applicable fee or $100 for facilities licensed by the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission where the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission license includes a water quality certification; or 2) the greater of either 50 percent of the applicable fee or $100 for all other hydropower facilities.

[page 4] [2] Pursuant to §1066(a)(3), the Annual Fee shall be 75 percent of the standard Permit and License Annual Fee based on the face value of the Permit or License, or a minimum fee of $300 plus $0.109 for every acre-foot in excess of 10 acre-feet actually diverted, whichever is greater, for Permits or Licenses issued on or after January 1, 2020 that meet the following criteria: (a) The Permit or License meets the criteria for the reduced application fee under footnote [8]; (b) The right holder has installed and is maintaining a stream gage with telemetry capabilities to provide publicly available real-time streamflow conditions via the California Data Exchange Center Website, and when available, a future website developed by the board; (c) The right holder timely submits the progress report by permittee required by section 925 or the report of licensee required by section 929.

[page 1] Wastewater Grants - Clean Water State Revolving Fund (CWSRF) Program Each state fiscal year (July – June) a new CWSRF Intended Use Plan (IUP) is adopted that describes the State Water Resources Control Board’s (State Water Board’s) plan for implanting the CWSRF and its complementary and supplementary funding sources.
This fact sheet specifically summarizes details for small community wastewater (SCWW) projects that serve disadvantaged communities per the 2026-27 CWSRF IUP, adopted July 7, 2026.
HELPFUL DEFINITIONS:
· Eligible Applicants: Public agencies having jurisdiction over disposal of sewage, industrial wastes, or other wastes, or an Indian tribe or an authorized Indian tribal organization, or a designated and approved management agency under Section 1288 of title 33 of the United States Code.
· Receiving System: means the publicly-owned treatment works (POTW) that provides wastewater treatment and disposal service to a Subsumed System through a Regionalization or septic-to-sewer project.
· Small Disadvantaged Community (Small DAC): means a community with a population of less than 20,000 and either or both of the following conditions:

[page 1] · Small Disadvantaged Community (Small DAC): means a community with a population of less than 20,000 and either or both of the following conditions:
o A median household income (MHI) of less than 80% of statewide MHI.
o A sewer rate of more than 4% of the community’s MHI.
· Small Community Wastewater (SCWW): grant/principal forgiveness (PF) and loan funding through the CWSRF program for projects benefiting small DACs.
· Subsumed System: means the POTW or, for septic-to-sewer projects, onsite sewage treatment system, consolidated into or receiving service from the Receiving System.

REGIONALIZATION INCENTIVES:
To encourage the regionalization of wastewater service, receiving systems (regardless of DAC status) may be eligible for a maximum of $10 million in 0% interest rate financing per connecting community/wastewater system for planning or construction of an Incentive Project. An Incentive Project is an eligible capital project that solely benefits the receiving system, and it is separate than funding for a regionalization project. Please refer to the CWSRF Intended Use Plan for more information.
IMPORTANT DEADLINES:

[page 3] IMPORTANT DEADLINES:
· Disbursements Year End Processing: At the end of each state fiscal year (July – June), DFA will experience a brief delay in payment processing due to year-end closure activities. To ensure your disbursement request is processed and paid within the current fiscal year, please submit complete and clean disbursement requests by March 31st. DFA will continue to process disbursement requests after March 31st, staging them for payment after July 1st.
· Complete Applications: To be considered in the next Fundable List update, applications shall be complete by September 30th.
· Amendment Requests: Recommend submitting complete amendment requests by December 31st. Amendment Requests after this date might experience delays due to year-end closure activities.

DRINKING WATER OPERATOR CERTIFICATION PROGRAM
Water Treatment and Water Distribution
Certification Fees
Once you receive notice that you have successfully passed the water distribution exam you may apply for certification. For grades 1-2, simply fill out the bottom portion of the notice and return it with the appropriate fee for the issuance of a certificate. Remember, you are not considered a certified operator until you apply and submit payment for the certificate.
For grades 3-5, a second application must be submitted, along with the certification fee and requisite documentation verifying experience requirements have been fulfilled.

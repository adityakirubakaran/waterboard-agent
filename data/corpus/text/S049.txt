Acceptable Continuing Education Topics
A “Continuing Education Course” is a presentation that transmits information related to the operation of a water treatment facility and/or distribution system. All classes, presentations and meetings must be a minimum of 50 minutes long. Multiple short classes over different days cannot be added together to equal one contact hour. Classes must be drinking water related;
general education classes are not acceptable. Wastewater classes are not acceptable.

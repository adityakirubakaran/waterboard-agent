## California Drinking Water-Related Statutes and Regulations
IMPORTANT NOTE: These documents are meant to aid staff of the Division of Drinking Water and should not be relied upon as the State of California's representation of the law. The published codes are the only official representation of the law and should be referenced whenever specific citations are required. Codes (statutes) can be accessed via the California Legislative Information website, and the Office of Administrative Law's website has information on how to obtain the official California Code of Regulations (CCR). Drinking water-related statutes are from the Corporations Code, Education Code, Food and Agricultural Code, Government Code, Health and Safety Code, Public Resources Code, and Water Code. Regulations are from Titles 17 and 22 of the CCR.
Note: Title 22, Chapter 12, Safe Drinking Water Project Funding and its associated regulations have been repealed and replaced by the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) Policy Handbook.
Note: Regulations pertaining to cross-connection control previously contained in Title 17, Division 1, Chapter 5, Subchapter 1, Group 4, Articles 1 and 2, have been repealed and replaced with a Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook.

- Drinking Water-Related Regulations - updated October 2026.
- Policy Handbooks - State Water Board policy handbooks having the force of regulation, but authorized by statute to be adopted via a streamlined public process not subject to the regular rulemaking requirements of Government Code section 11340 et seq.
- Policy for Implementing the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) - amended May 2025; for more information contact the Division of Financial Assistance.
- Standard Method of Testing and Reporting of Microplastics in Drinking Water - adopted September 2022
- Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook: Standards and Principles for California's Public Water Systems - updated April 2026
Before citing any statute or regulation, the text of the statute or regulation should be confirmed by visiting the website or the official publications mentioned above. Please report any discrepancies between these documents and the published codes to Melissa.Hall@waterboards.ca.gov.

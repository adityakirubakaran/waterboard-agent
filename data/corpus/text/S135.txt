[page 1] Filing a Petition with the Division of Water Rights A petition is needed to make changes to a water right permit, license, or a pending application. To make a change to a water right registration, please refer to the Registrations Program website. Below you will find help on where to begin the petition process in CalWATRS.
Accessing Your Water Rights
You will need to have created a CalWATRS account and migrated your records in order to file a petition for your water rights. If you have not created an account yet you can find help here.
1. Create a CalWATRS account. If you need help with this step, please review this guide: waterboards.ca.gov/upward/calwatrs/resources/creating-an-account.pdf.
2. Log in to your CalWATRS account.
3. Use the following link to access the “Petition” page:
https://calwatrs.waterboards.ca.gov/portal/s/petition?c__isRequestChange=No.
a. To file a wastewater change petition, please see page 4 below.
4. Click the “Start Petition” button.

5. Review and agree to the notice.
6. You can search for your water right ID and select the petition type. Click “Next” and complete the remaining pages. Note, throughout the petition, you can save your progress and return later by clicking “Save and exit”.
7. Upon petition submittal, you will receive an email confirmation. Once both your complete petition and appropriate fee(s) are received, your petition will be reviewed by State Water Board staff.
